Study – Support

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Study is an AI study companion from Rework. Chat with a tutor, add your notes, photos, documents, recordings and links, and turn them into lessons, flashcards, quizzes, mock exams and revision plans.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: sebastian@rework.no. We usually reply within a few working days.

Deleting your data

  • Study library: delete individual items or whole topics in the app. Flashcards made from a topic are deleted with it. Deleting a flashcard deck also removes its cards from our servers.
  • Journal and settings on this device: Settings › Your Data › Delete all local data. You can export your journal first from the same screen.
  • Your whole account and everything on our servers (chats, uploaded files, lessons, progress): email sebastian@rework.no with the subject "Delete my Study data". Study accounts are anonymous, so please tell us something you added (for example a topic or file name and roughly when) so we can find your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the app removes the data on your device, but not data stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Is Study free?

Yes. Study is currently free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. To keep it running for everyone, there are daily limits on AI features such as tutor messages, uploads and generated lessons. They reset every day.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Study uses OpenAI's API to power the tutor. Under OpenAI's API terms, data submitted via the API is not used to train OpenAI's models, and we don't train models on your data either. Your private journal never leaves your device.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is needed. Study creates an anonymous account for your installation. There is currently no way to move it to a new device, so deleting the app or changing phone means starting fresh.

Who is Study for?

Study is for learners aged 13 and over. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent or guardian before using it.

Can I trust the tutor's answers?

The tutor is helpful but can make mistakes. Check important facts against your course material and teachers, and follow your school's rules on using AI.

The tutor says I've reached today's limit.

Daily limits reset the next day (UTC). You can still review your flashcards and use your journal in the meantime.
