Study – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Study app ("Study", "the app") processes, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We have tried to write it plainly. If anything is unclear, email us at sebastian@rework.no.

1. Who we are

Study is made by Rework, the studio of Sebastian Oltedal Thorp, based in Norway (rework.no). Rework is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: sebastian@rework.no

2. The short version

  • You don't create an account with a name, email or phone number. Study gives your installation an anonymous account.
  • The study material you add (messages to the tutor, photos, documents, recordings, notes and links) is stored on our servers so the app can work, and is sent to OpenAI to generate the tutor's answers, lessons, flashcards and quizzes.
  • Your private journal stays on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers or sent to AI.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we don't sell your data.

3. What we collect

3.1 Anonymous account

The first time you use the tutor or other online features, Study creates an anonymous account for your installation. We store a random account identifier and the session tokens needed to keep you signed in. The tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or any sign-in with Apple, Google or other services.

3.2 Study content you add

When you use the online features, we store the following on our servers, linked to your anonymous account:

  • Tutor chats – the messages you send, the tutor's replies, the subject and level you picked, and references to files you attached.
  • Files – photos (for example of notes or homework), PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files, text files, audio recordings and video files you upload. We keep the original file and the text read from it.
  • Notes and links – notes you type and links you add to your study library (including YouTube links), with the text and captions read from them.
  • Generated study material – lessons, quizzes, flashcards, podcasts, mock exams, summaries and revision plans made for you.
  • Exam and plan details – exam dates and names, and how many minutes a day you want to study, if you enter them.
  • Answers and progress – your answers to mock exams (typed, or the transcript of a spoken answer), scores, time spent, flashcard and quiz reviews (including how sure you said you were) and spaced-repetition scheduling data.
  • Usage counters – how many messages, uploads and other AI requests you made today, so we can apply daily limits.

3.3 Voice

When you dictate a message or answer an oral exam out loud, the recording is sent to our servers and on to OpenAI to be turned into text. We keep the resulting text (for example as a chat message or exam answer), not the recording. Audio files you deliberately upload as study material are stored like other files.

When the app reads text aloud, the text is sent to OpenAI to create speech. The resulting audio clips are cached on our servers for up to 30 days so replays are instant; these clips are not linked to your account. In some cases the app instead uses your device's built-in speech, which happens entirely on the device.

Where live voice conversation with the tutor is available, your microphone audio is streamed directly from your device to OpenAI for the duration of the conversation, and a one-way hashed version of your account identifier is sent to OpenAI to help it detect abuse without learning who you are.

3.4 Kept only on your device

The following stays in the app's local storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your private journal, including entries, mood and energy check-ins, tags, photos and voice notes.
  • Your onboarding answers such as your age range, goals, name (if you enter one), and app settings and reminders.
  • Your Face ID / fingerprint unlock. Biometric checks are handled by your device's operating system; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Some onboarding choices (your subjects, level, preferred tutor tone and voice) are sent along with tutor requests so the tutor can adapt to you. Flashcard decks you create are kept on your device and copied to our servers when you start reviewing them, so your reviews can be scheduled.

3.5 Technical data

When your device connects to our servers, our hosting provider necessarily processes technical data such as your IP address and request metadata to deliver the service and keep it secure. We don't store your IP address in our database or use it to identify you.

3.6 What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, health data, payment details, advertising identifiers or device identifiers, and we don't use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. Please don't include sensitive personal information (for example about your health) in material you share with the tutor; we don't ask for it.

4. Why we use your data and our legal bases

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. AI-generated grades and feedback in the app are study aids only.

5. AI processing by OpenAI

Study uses OpenAI's API to power its AI features. Depending on what you do, we send OpenAI:

  • your recent tutor messages and attached photos or PDFs, with your subject, level and chosen tone, to generate replies and suggestions;
  • text, photos, PDFs and transcripts from your study material, to read them and to generate summaries, lessons, flashcards, quizzes, mock exams and revision plans;
  • your mock-exam answers, to grade them and give feedback;
  • voice recordings and audio or video files, to transcribe them;
  • text to be read aloud, to generate speech;
  • the title of a study topic or video, so OpenAI's web search can suggest further videos and articles or describe a video whose captions can't be read.

OpenAI acts as our data processor. We ask OpenAI not to store responses for later retrieval, and under OpenAI's API terms, data submitted via the API is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI may keep API data for a limited period to monitor for abuse, as described in its own policies. You can read more in OpenAI's privacy policy.

AI output can be wrong. See section 8 of our Terms of Use.

6. Other service providers

  • Convex (Convex, Inc.) hosts our backend: database, file storage, sign-in and scheduled jobs. Convex acts as our data processor.
  • Websites you link to. When you add a web link, our servers fetch that page to read its text. The website sees a request from our servers, not from your device.
  • YouTube. When you add a YouTube link on your phone, the app requests the video's captions directly from YouTube, and video thumbnails load from YouTube. YouTube (Google) therefore receives your device's IP address and the video you added, under Google's privacy policy. Images for suggested articles may likewise load from the website that published them.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through their app stores and process data under their own policies when you download it.

We don't sell your personal data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

7. International transfers

Our Convex database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Convex, Inc. and OpenAI are based in the United States, and OpenAI processes data in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on the safeguards provided in our providers' data processing terms, namely the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can contact us for more information about these safeguards.

8. Device permissions

  • Camera and photos – to take or choose photos of your notes or homework for the tutor, and to add photos to your private journal (journal photos stay on your device).
  • Microphone – to talk to your tutor, dictate, answer oral exams and record journal voice notes (journal voice notes stay on your device).
  • Face ID / biometrics – to lock your private journal, if you turn this on.
  • Notifications – for study reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we don't use push notification servers or collect a push token.
  • Files – when you pick a document, the app only gets access to the files you choose.

All permissions are optional. You can change them at any time in your device settings; the related features will then stop working.

9. How long we keep your data

  • Account and study content: kept while your account exists, until you delete it in the app (where available) or ask us to delete it.
  • Daily usage counters: deleted after 3 days.
  • Read-aloud audio clips: deleted after 30 days.
  • Voice recordings for dictation and spoken answers: not stored by us; only the text is kept.

Your anonymous account is tied to your app installation. There is currently no account recovery or sync between devices. If you delete the app or clear its data, you will lose access to your account, but the data stored on our servers is not automatically deleted. If you want it deleted, contact us (see section 10). If we introduce automatic deletion of inactive accounts, we will describe it here first.

10. Deleting your data

  • In the app: you can delete items and whole topics from your study library (this also deletes the flashcards made from them), and deleting a flashcard deck removes its cards from our servers. Settings › Your Data › Delete all local data deletes your journal and preferences from your device; it does not delete data stored on our servers.
  • Everything else: email sebastian@rework.no with the subject "Delete my Study data". Because Study doesn't know your name or email, we can't find your account from your email address alone. Please describe something you added (for example a topic name or file name, and roughly when you added it) so we can locate your account. We will delete your account and its server data, including chats, uploaded files, lessons and progress, within 30 days, and confirm by email.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR (which applies in Norway through the Personal Data Act) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to our processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To use these rights, email sebastian@rework.no. We'll reply within one month. As explained above, we may need details of your content to identify your anonymous account.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet; you can also contact the authority where you live or work.

12. Children

Study is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you are under the age at which you can consent to online services in your country (13 in Norway, and up to 16 in some EU countries), you need a parent's or guardian's permission to use Study. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Study, contact us and we'll delete the data.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app, our servers and OpenAI (HTTPS). Session tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. Our servers check that every request comes from the account that owns the data, and the AI provider's secret key never leaves our servers. Your journal can be locked with Face ID or your device's authentication. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
